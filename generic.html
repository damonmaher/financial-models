About Us

Two students building financial models and writing up what we learn.

What this is

This site is a joint project between us, Damon Maher and Alec Mahini. We're building financial models on our own time and posting them here as we go, mostly things related to market regimes, volatility, and structural shifts.

We try to keep everything on this site interpretable. If we build something and can't explain plainly how it works, we don't consider it finished.

How we got here

We're both students with an interest in systematic trading and market structure. This started with a Hidden Markov Model we built for regime detection, and it grew from there into an ongoing project.

We're planning to keep adding models over time. If you're a trader, an analyst, or a club recruiter looking through student work, we hope there's something useful here.
