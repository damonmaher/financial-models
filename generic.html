About Us

Quantitative research, regime detection, and systematic financial modeling.

Our Mission

Mahini & Maher Research and Models is a collaborative project between Damon Maher and Alec Mahini. Our work focuses on building transparent, practical, and academically grounded financial models. We aim to bridge the gap between theoretical quantitative finance and real‑world decision‑making by creating tools that identify market regimes, structural shifts, and volatility patterns.

Our philosophy is simple: models should be interpretable, robust, and useful. Whether it’s regime detection, macro‑state classification, or volatility structure analysis, we prioritize clarity and operational value over complexity for its own sake.

Our Background

We are two researchers with a shared interest in quantitative finance, systematic trading, and macro‑market structure. Our collaboration began with the development of a Hidden Markov Model (HMM) for regime detection — a project that quickly evolved into a broader research initiative.

As we continue to expand our library of models, our goal is to build a suite of tools that help traders, analysts, and portfolio managers better understand the underlying dynamics of financial markets.

What We’re Building

Our current and upcoming projects include:

Hidden Markov Model (HMM)
