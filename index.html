Mahini & Maher Research and Models

Quantitative financial modeling, regime detection, and systematic research.

Who We Are

We are Damon Maher and Alec Mahini — two researchers focused on building practical, transparent, and high‑signal financial models. Our work centers on regime detection, volatility structure, macro‑state classification, and systematic decision frameworks.

Our goal is to create models that are both academically grounded and operationally useful for traders, analysts, and portfolio managers.

Learn more

Hidden Markov Model (HMM)

Our first major project is a Hidden Markov Model designed for market regime detection. The model identifies latent market states — such as trending, mean‑reverting, or volatile regimes — using probabilistic inference.

The explanation page breaks down the math, intuition, and implementation details.
